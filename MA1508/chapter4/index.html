MA1508 Calculus · Chapter 4

Applications of Differentiation

Calculus for first-year data-science students. Stewart, Single Variable Calculus, 4th edition, is the mathematical backbone; short data-science connections provide background rather than replacing the core examples.

Learning route

Learn the definition and theorem conditions, work through a standard example, then try the practice question before opening its answer. The optional background cards need not be taught before students can solve the core calculus problems.

Our course numbering

Rates of Change deliberately opens this chapter as 4.1 (Stewart §3.8). Related Rates follows as 4.1′ (Stewart §4.1). The MVT is placed in 4.2 to prepare for curve analysis; Stewart treats it in §4.3. Stewart §4.4 curve-graphing guidance is included in our 4.3 rather than a separate page.

Prerequisite: Chapter 3 differentiation rules. Angles are in radians unless stated otherwise.
