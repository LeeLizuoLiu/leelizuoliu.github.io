MA1508 Calculus · Chapter 5

Integrals

Five lectures following Stewart, Single Variable Calculus: Concepts and Contexts, 4th edition. Start with antiderivatives, construct definite integrals from finite sums, then develop integration techniques, numerical integration and improper integrals.

The five lectures

5.1–5.3Antiderivatives and Definite IntegralsAntiderivatives and initial conditions (Stewart §4.8), followed by area, definite integrals and net change.5.4–5.6The Fundamental Theorem and Integration TechniquesConnect accumulation with differentiation, then reverse the chain rule and product rule.5.7Additional Techniques of IntegrationTrigonometric identities and substitutions; partial fractions.5.8–5.9Numerical IntegrationApproximate integrals with midpoint, trapezoidal and Simpson rules, using formulas or measured data.5.10Improper IntegralsInfinite tails, endpoint singularities and convergence tests.
